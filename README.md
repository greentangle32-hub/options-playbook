## Built for your AI assistant

This is the part no other options book does.

**Every lesson of the Options Playbook ships as structured Markdown + JSON** — not just a PDF. Each lesson file carries concept IDs, worked examples, and misconception notes, and the Full Edition adds:

- A **110-question scenario quiz bank** — every wrong answer names the misconception behind it, with spaced repetition on days 3 and 7
- A **daily teaching protocol** — one lesson with your morning briefing, a second lesson and quiz in the evening, and a preview of tomorrow

Drop the files into your own AI assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or a local model — and it teaches and quizzes you, lesson by lesson, at your pace.

### How to use the AI study pack

1. Download the **free Starter** below — you get the visual PDF plus the Starter AI pack (Markdown + JSON).
2. Open your AI assistant and upload the lesson `.md` files (or the combined knowledge-pack file).
3. Say: *"Teach me Lesson 1, then quiz me."* Follow the daily teaching protocol for a morning-lesson / evening-quiz rhythm.
4. Your assistant tracks the concept IDs, so it can revisit exactly what you missed — days 3 and 7 are built in for review.

Want to see the format first? The [`sample/`](sample/) folder in this repo holds two real lessons from the free Starter AI pack — Lesson 1 and Lesson 8 (The Greeks) — in Markdown. Feed them to your AI right now and try it.
